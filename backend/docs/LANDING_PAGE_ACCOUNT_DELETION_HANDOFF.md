# Landing page — account deletion (Google Play handoff)

**Public URL:** `https://<domain>/account-deletion`  
**Footer link label:** Delete account → `/account-deletion`

## Requirement

The website must explain how users delete their Platio account. Deletion is **only** available in the mobile app; the landing page does not perform deletion (Google Play disclosure).

## In-app steps (match live customer app UI)

1. Open the Platio app and sign in.
2. Tap **Profile** (bottom tab).
3. Scroll past **Rate Your Meals**, **Meal History**, **Billing & Wallet**, **Refer Friends**, and **Delivery Location**.
4. In the **Account** section, directly above **Sign out**, tap **Delete account**.
5. Confirm in the dialog. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

**Profile order (reference):** settings list → **Account** → **Delete account** → **Sign out** (separate row; signing out is not the same as deleting).

## Footer hint (optional)

Delete your account in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account (above Sign out).

## Data removed when you delete

- Profile and contact information (name, email, mobile number, profile photo).
- Login credentials and active app sessions (you can no longer sign in with that account).
- Saved delivery location and in-app meal preferences tied to your account.
- Active subscription enrollment in the app (no further scheduled deliveries under that account).
- Push notification registrations and marketing opt-in preferences for that account.
- Voluntary dietary or allergen preferences stored on your profile.

## Data we may keep after deletion

We delete or anonymize personal data when it is no longer needed, but we may retain certain records where required by Saudi law or for legitimate purposes, including:

- Order, invoice, and payment transaction records required for tax (ZATCA), accounting, and commercial record-keeping.
- Masked or tokenized payment references held by our payment providers (we do not store full card numbers).
- Food-safety and traceability records linked to orders you placed (SFDA and related requirements).
- Information needed to resolve disputes, chargebacks, fraud prevention, or legal claims.
- Aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you.

Retention periods follow our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) and applicable regulations.
